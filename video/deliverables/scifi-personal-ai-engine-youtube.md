Everyone is watching the benchmark scores. That's the wrong thing to watch.

Every technology that reshaped the economy did it the same way: the velvet rope
of specialized expertise dropped, costs fell, the stakes went to zero, and
ordinary people started experimenting. The printing press didn't just sell more
books — it sparked the scientific revolution. The automobile didn't just sell
more cars — it invented the suburbs. AI is at that same moment right now, and
the question that matters isn't how fast the engine spins.

This episode walks through what to actually do about it: the six skills that
turn a generic model into your own personal AI operating system, and why the
proprietary value was never in the model.

• Why human taste becomes the bottleneck when volume is free

• The vending machine and the slot machine: when NOT to use generative AI
• How a personal workflow clones the output of a five-person team
